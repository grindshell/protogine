M2 Phase 0 feasibility probe
Windows MSVC x64, rustc 1.95.0 (59807616e 2026-04-14), against commit 78e8b7e

cargo build --release --example tilemap_m2_probe --no-default-features

Prototype geometry over a Vec<TileMap>, not the production kernel. The work
mode's two arms are equal by construction - a Vec index has no per-map cost to
grow - so that pair is a reference value for Phase 2 and not evidence on its
own. What does guard the arrangement is the per-body geometry prediction, which
an equality cannot replace: a uniformly added per-body term leaves both arms
equal.

--- The probe's own assertions, watched failing -------------------------------

Each breakage was applied to examples/tilemap_m2_probe.rs, built, run, and
reverted; the tree was confirmed byte-identical after each.

  spread arm all on one map             exit=101  every map must carry its share of bodies
  bodies that do not move               exit=101  every body must charge something
  every body identical                  exit=101  per-body cost must vary
  one map short of the aggregate        exit=101  the maximum map count must be built
  a shape claimed to fit that does not  exit=101  does not behave as the contract claims
  one unit per body for membership      exit=101  every body must charge exactly what its
                                                  geometry predicts, and body 0 did not
                                                  left: 1905  right: 1904

The sixth passes all five of the others. Before the prediction existed, a
per-body overhead of up to 788 units each - 52% of a body's average cost of
1,435 - passed every assertion in the work mode, because the only bound was the
arrangement's worst case of 2,359,296 against a measured 1,469,888.
