M2 Phase 0 feasibility probe

cargo build --release --example tilemap_m2_probe --no-default-features

Prototype geometry over a Vec<TileMap>, not the production kernel. The work
mode's two arms are equal by construction - a Vec index has no per-map cost to
grow - so that pair is a reference value for Phase 2 and not evidence on its
own. What guards the arrangement is the per-body geometry prediction, which an
equality cannot replace: a uniformly added per-body term leaves both arms equal.

Run at the owner's map count of 64, raised from a proposed 32 on this probe's
own evidence. The comparison shape is derived from that count - it must be the
largest shape whose 64 copies fit the aggregate - so it is 128x64 rather than
the 128x128 the 32-map version used.

--- The probe's own assertions, watched failing -------------------------------

Each breakage applied to examples/tilemap_m2_probe.rs, built, run, reverted;
the tree confirmed byte-identical after each. All eight re-run at 64 maps.

  spread arm all on one map             exit=101  every map must carry its share of bodies
  bodies that do not move               exit=101  every body must charge something
  every body identical                  exit=101  per-body cost must vary
  one map short of the aggregate        exit=101  the maximum map count must be built
  a shape claimed to fit that does not  exit=101  does not behave as the contract claims
  one unit per body for membership      exit=101  every body must charge exactly what its
                                                  geometry predicts, and body 0 did not
  column variation lost (i%1)           exit=101  every body must start somewhere different
  moduli changed, spread intact (i%23)  exit=101  the arrangement's total is pinned

The sixth passes all five of the others. Without the prediction, a per-body
overhead of up to 609 units each - 54% of a body's average cost of 1,119 -
passed every assertion, because the only bound was the arrangement's worst case
of 1,769,472 against a measured 1,145,600.

--- Cross-check against M1 -----------------------------------------------------

  M1 Phase 2 long sweep : 11,386,880 units, p50 16.5-17.8 ms
  M2 Phase 0 arrangement:  1,145,600 units, p50 1.783-1.934 ms over nine runs
  ratio of units 9.94; that p50 range gives 17.7-19.2 ms, median 17.8 - at the
  top of M1's range rather than inside it, and above it on a slow run.

  The run-to-run spread is 8.5%, the same order as the movement between the
  three arrangements this was measured against, so the claim holds only at the
  order of magnitude: a tenth of the units takes roughly a tenth of the time.
  An observation, not a model. Measuring it properly made it weaker.
