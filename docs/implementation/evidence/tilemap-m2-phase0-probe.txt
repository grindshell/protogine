Prototype geometry over a Vec<TileMap>, not the production kernel. The work
mode's two arms are equal by construction - a Vec index has no per-map cost to
grow - so that pair is a reference value for Phase 2 and not evidence on its
own. The storage, shapes and timing figures are measurements.

--- The probe's own configuration assertions, watched failing ----------------

Each breakage was applied to examples/tilemap_m2_probe.rs, built, run, and
reverted; the tree was confirmed byte-identical afterwards. A mode that reports
a plausible number while measuring the wrong arrangement is this plan's most
frequent defect, so these are the assertions that matter most here.

  spread arm all on one map            exit=101  fired at: every map must carry its share of bodies
  bodies that do not move              exit=101  fired at: every body must charge something
  every body identical                 exit=101  fired at: per-body cost must vary
  one map short of the aggregate       exit=101  fired at: the maximum map count must be built
  a shape claimed to fit that does not exit=101  fired at: does not behave as the contract claims
